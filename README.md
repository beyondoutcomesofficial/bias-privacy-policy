# Privacy Policy — Bias

**Effective Date:** May 15, 2026
**Developer:** R Satyamoorthy
**Contact:** privacy@bias.app

## Overview

Bias is a daily learning app for iOS. The app collects no personal data and stores everything locally on your device.

## Data Collection

Bias collects no personal data. There is no account creation, no login, and no registration of any kind. All app data — including journal entries, completed lessons, streak count, and progress — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store. The developer does not collect, store, or have access to your payment information. Manage or cancel any time via Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

## Notifications

Bias sends one local daily notification as a reminder. It is generated on your device and not transmitted to any server. Turn off any time in Settings > Notifications > Bias.
